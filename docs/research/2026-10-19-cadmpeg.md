# cadmpeg as a CAD backend (2026-10-19)

Checked on 2026-10-19 against github.com/cadmpeg/cadmpeg and crates.io. Re-check before relying
on versions or support levels: the project moves fast.

## Facts

- Rust, Apache-2.0 code, CC BY 4.0 docs. Created 2026-07-10, release v0.6.2 on 2026-10-19,
  158 stars. MSRV 1.88
- Split into crates: `cadmpeg-ir` (data model, validation, diffing, codec API) and one
  `cadmpeg-codec-*` crate per format, plus `cadmpeg` (CLI). Rezzer can depend on the IR and the
  codecs it needs, without the CLI
- Clean-room policy: format knowledge from legally owned files and public docs only, no vendor
  SDKs or decompiled binaries (their LEGAL.md). DCO and provenance declarations for decoder changes
- Transitive licenses of `cadmpeg-ir` plus the FreeCAD, STEP, Fusion, SolidWorks, Creo and
  Inventor codecs (89 packages): all MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause, Zlib, Unlicense or 0BSD.
  `r-efi` is MIT OR Apache-2.0 OR LGPL-2.1, so the MIT option applies. Nothing blocks MPL-2.0
- They collect a public CC0 test corpus (corpus/README.md). A possible source of CAD test files
  for the CAD spike; check each file's license first

## Support ladder (their own scoring, 2026-10-19)

L0 parsed, L1 opened (sections, versions, assets, external references, named undecoded content),
L2 geometry, L3 B-rep model, L4 design records (features with operation semantics, sketches,
history), L5 shape complete, L6 design complete (constraints, dimensions, parameters,
expressions, configurations), L7 product (assemblies, mates), L8 full document (PMI, drawings),
L9 writes back.

| Format | Level |
|---|---|
| STEP Part 21 | L9 |
| IGES | L9 |
| FreeCAD `.FCStd` | L5 |
| Fusion 360 `.f3d` | L4 |
| Inventor `.ipt`/`.iam`, SolidWorks `.sldprt`, Creo `.prt`, NX `.prt`, CATIA V5 `.CATPart`, Rhino `.3dm`, ACIS/ASM `.sat` | L1 (some with partial extras, for example Inventor parameters and simple features) |

## Fit for Rezzer

Strong on semantics, which is what Rezzer is judged by:

- The IR has design arenas: `features` (with construction order), `parameters`, `sketches`,
  `sketch_constraints`, `configurations`, `spreadsheets`, `product_definitions`, `occurrences`,
  `assembly_joints`, `pmi`, `semantic_annotations`, `attributes`, `appearances`
- Every unsupported construct produces a machine-readable loss, and unknown records stay named.
  This maps directly to Rezzer's rule to say what it could not read
- `decode_depth` (container, geometry, full) tells how much was transferred
- Stable entity IDs (`<format>:<scope>:<kind>#<key>`) could become anchors in the Markdown

What it gives Rezzer per format: FreeCAD and Fusion feature trees and parameters (Mermaid feature
tree, parameter tables), STEP product structure and PMI, and for Creo, SolidWorks, NX, CATIA and
Inventor more than "metadata only" (structure, versions, external references, assets), but still
no reliable geometry or feature history. The advice to ask for a STEP export stays for those.

## Gaps

No DXF, DWG, 3MF, STL, OBJ, glTF, IFC, KiCad, Gerber, G-code or OpenSCAD. cadmpeg adds to the crate
list in 2026-10-16-cad.md for native MCAD formats; it does not replace it.

## Risks

- Three months old, near-daily changes, IR at version 6 already. Pin exactly, keep it behind
  Rezzer's CAD reader trait, keep reference files with expected output
- Binary size and build time not measured yet. Measure in the CAD spike
- Their headline levels are self-scored; the CAD spike should check them on real files
